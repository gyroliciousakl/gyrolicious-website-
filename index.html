Our story

Street food with Greek magic.

Gyrolicious is built around generous food, warm hospitality and the flavours that make Greek street food unforgettable.

From sizzling grilled meat to warm house-made pita, crisp salad, tzatziki and punchy sumac onion, we keep things fresh, satisfying and full of character. Find us in Hillcrest, or bring the truck to your own celebration.
